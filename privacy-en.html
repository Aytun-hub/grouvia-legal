Grouvia Privacy Policy

Last updated: 26 October 2026

Grouvia is a mobile app that helps groups of friends decide together on places, dates, tasks and shared expenses. This policy explains what personal data we process, why, where it is stored, and what rights you have.

1. Data controller and contact

The data controller is the developer of Grouvia, Aytun VURAL (Turkey).

  • Contact e-mail: atnvrl@protonmail.com
  • Send any question about this policy or your data to this address.

2. Data we collect

  • Account information: when you sign in with Google, the name and e-mail address of your Google account. We never see your password.
  • Group content: group name, invite code, membership, place and date suggestions, votes, comments, tasks, expenses, and the name of the person who added them.
  • IBAN / payment details (optional): the group admin may enter an IBAN or other payment details where contributions should be sent. This is shown only to members of that group.
  • Payment status: the "pending / awaiting confirmation / confirmed" marks members set inside a group.
  • Technical data: Google/Firebase, which provides sign-in and the database, may keep technical logs such as IP address and device information on its own systems for security and to operate the service.

What we do not collect: your location (GPS), contacts, camera, microphone or advertising ID. The app contains no ads and no analytics tools. We do not sell your personal data or share it for advertising.

Important: Grouvia is not a payment app. No money is transferred through the app and no bank or card details are collected. IBANs and payment statuses are for information inside the group only. The "Open in map" button opens an address in your own map app and does not share your location with us.

3. Why we use your data

  • To let you sign in and identify you to the members of your groups (name and e-mail).
  • To let group members decide together on places, dates, tasks and expenses.
  • To let the group admin manage members (invite, remove, approve).
  • To keep the service secure and prevent abuse.

Legal bases (GDPR Art. 6): performance of a contract, i.e. providing the app to you (Art. 6(1)(b)), and our legitimate interests in security and preventing abuse (Art. 6(1)(f)). Entering an IBAN or payment details is entirely optional and is something you choose to share. For users in Turkey the equivalent bases under KVKK art. 5/2 apply.

4. Who sees your data and where it is stored

  • Group members: once you join a group, your name, e-mail, the content you add, your votes and your comments are visible to the other members of that group. People in other groups cannot see them.
  • Service provider: your data is stored with Google's Firebase services (Authentication and Cloud Firestore). The database is located in Google's data centres in Europe (Belgium and the Netherlands).
  • International transfers: Google may process some technical data outside your country, including outside the EEA, under the safeguards it provides, such as standard contractual clauses.
  • Legal requests: we do not share your data with third parties except where a competent authority lawfully requires it.

5. Retention

Your data is kept for as long as your account and your group exist. When you delete your account or a group, the related data is deleted (details below). Backup copies on the provider's systems are removed within a reasonable time under the provider's own deletion processes.

6. Deleting your account and data

In the app

  1. Open the My groups screen.
  2. Open the three-dot menu at the top right and choose Delete my account.
  3. Read the warning, confirm, and verify by signing in with Google again.

When your account is deleted, your Google sign-in record and group memberships are removed and your votes and comments are deleted. Shared content you added to groups (suggestions, tasks, expenses) may remain as shared group data. If you do not want that, delete the items you added before deleting your account.

Deleting a group or leaving a group

If the group founder deletes a group, the group and everything in it is deleted for everyone. A founder can instead transfer the admin role to another member and leave; the group then continues for the others. If a founder deletes their account and other members remain, the admin role passes automatically to the longest-standing member; if nobody else remains, the group is deleted. Members can leave a group at any time.

By e-mail

If you cannot access the app, write to atnvrl@protonmail.com from the e-mail address of your Google account with the subject "Delete my account". We will complete your request within 30 days at the latest. See the Account & Data Deletion page for details.

7. Your rights

Depending on where you live (for example under the GDPR in the EU/EEA and the UK, or KVKK in Turkey), you can ask us to:

  • tell you whether we process your personal data and give you a copy of it (access, portability),
  • correct data that is inaccurate or incomplete,
  • delete your data ("right to be forgotten"),
  • restrict or object to certain processing,
  • tell you who your data has been shared with, including abroad.

Send your request to atnvrl@protonmail.com; we reply within 30 days. If you are not satisfied with our answer, you have the right to complain to your local data protection authority (in Turkey, the Personal Data Protection Board).

8. Security, children and changes

Security: your data is stored on Google's secure infrastructure with access rules that allow only signed-in users who are members of a group to read that group's data. No system can be guaranteed 100% secure.

Children: Grouvia is not directed at children under 13 and we do not knowingly collect data from them. If you notice this has happened, tell us and we will delete the data.

Changes: we may update this policy from time to time and will announce important changes on this page. The date at the top is the date of the last update.
