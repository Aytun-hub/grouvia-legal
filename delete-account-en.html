Grouvia: Account & Data Deletion

App: Grouvia · Developer: Aytun VURAL · Last updated: 26 October 2026

You can delete your Grouvia account and the personal data linked to it at any time. There are two ways.

Option 1: In the app (recommended)

  1. Open Grouvia and go to the My groups screen.
  2. Tap the three-dot menu at the top right and choose Delete my account.
  3. Read the warning and tap Delete permanently.
  4. Sign in with your Google account again to confirm.

The deletion happens immediately and cannot be undone.

Option 2: By e-mail

If you cannot access the app, send an e-mail with the subject "Delete my account" to atnvrl@protonmail.com, from the e-mail address of the Google account you use for Grouvia. The request must come from that address so we can verify it is you. We complete requests within 30 days at the latest and confirm by e-mail.

What is deleted

  • Your Google sign-in record (name and e-mail).
  • All your group memberships and your "My groups" list.
  • Your votes and comments in groups you are a member of.
  • In groups you founded: if other members remain, the admin role passes to the longest-standing member and the group continues for them; if nobody else remains, the group is deleted with all its content.

What may remain

  • Shared content you added to groups (place and date suggestions, tasks, expenses) may remain as shared group data. If you do not want that, delete those items in the app before deleting your account.
  • Backup and log copies on Google's systems are removed within a reasonable time under the provider's own deletion processes.

Only deleting a group

To remove a group without deleting your account, tap the bin icon next to the group on the My groups screen. If you are the founder you can transfer the admin role to another member and leave, or delete the group for everyone. If you are a member, you leave the group.

For the full picture of your rights, see the Privacy Policy.
